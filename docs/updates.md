## 2026-10-12 — Beta-board runtime, licensing and hardware-loop progress / Beta 板运行时、许可与真机控制环进展

- Reviewed official stable releases [v0.15.3](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.15.3), [v0.16.0](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.16.0) and [v0.16.1](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.16.1). Together they add board-selected hardware support, the beta board's ToF, head IMU, NFC, camera orientation and face LEDs, battery display, a safer skill/bring-up state machine, and sitting the robot when its gamepad drops out. The v0.15.3 notes explicitly say that build was CI-verified but not robot-validated; later `daemon-dev-*` artifacts remain prereleases.
- Recorded the official [Microduck RL license clarification](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/commit/7cc1961674f78d4fca7064fde715e0c9bcd7b9c4). The listed bundled STL meshes are now explicitly CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; code, MJCF XML, export configs and other files remain Apache-2.0. This is a scope clarification, not a new training capability.
- Updated [MicroDuck ROS 2 + Isaac Sim](https://github.com/osrbot/microduck-ros2-isaac/commit/2adc4b157fc732d4751ad97577e4ea39bd727abd). Its bilingual tutorials and source pins now follow the October upstream and immutable v7 policy set. The project explicitly keeps its converted USD and native training inputs on the historical baseline until GPU playback is retested, and still has no physical driver.
- Updated [Microduck replica](https://github.com/fanhao375/microduck-replica/commit/75c2b4b3249a8a4a17e29dabb872ebb465c393f9). The maintainer reports a BNO085 path, all 15 Feetech servos responding at the 50 Hz loop, HOME, and a ten-second policy hold on the physical build. The web-joystick walking attempt was blocked by the board throttling at 92 °C, so continuous walking remains unverified.
- Added [MicroDuck Jetson Orin Nano mount](https://github.com/adammong/microduck-jetson-mount/commit/8d361ceae40c8474e43468bfbd2d311344387fa1), a parametric external-compute and battery mount with printable CAD, wiring studies, MuJoCo payload models, published policies and reproducible regression tables. Its own reports reject some candidate controllers and state that physical fit, strength and control are untested, so it is labeled `simulation-reported`.
- Deferred [my-microduck-journal](https://github.com/xinhuagu/my-microduck-journal) despite detailed WSL2 training scripts and fixed-seed simulation tables because its checkpoints and ONNX files are not published yet. Deferred [Microduck-kissqy](https://github.com/kissqy/Microduck-kissqy) as a separate catalog entry because the newly described firmware, image and training console are explicitly marked upcoming; its published hardware videos and the independent replica bring-up remain useful evidence. Also deferred quackd's post-v0.17.1 Microduck compatibility work until it is released, and new HD-1910/DIY repositories that lacked a real-motion demonstration, complete public artifacts, or stable licensing/provenance.
- Rechecked the four official Microduck Arena reference-model repositories. Their model files remain public, but the claimed `pollen-robotics/microduck-challenges` reproduction repository still returns 404, so they remain deferred as separate entries.

本次核心更新：官方稳定运行时升至 v0.16.1，补齐 beta 板传感器、摄像头、灯效与手柄断连安全行为；官方训练仓明确 STL 网格采用 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0，其余列明文件保持 Apache-2.0。ROS 2 路线已对齐 v7 策略但仍无真机驱动；HD-1910 复刻已在实体机上跑通 BNO085、15 舵机控制环、HOME 与 10 秒策略保持，连续行走仍因过热降频未验证。新增 Jetson Orin Nano 外置算力挂载方案，但仅按仿真结果标注。缺少公开权重、尚未发布新版、未发版兼容修订或复现仓仍不可访问的候选继续暂缓收录。

## 2026-10-08 — Stable runtime, policy refresh and safer community routes / 稳定运行时、策略更新与社区路线安全修订

- Reviewed official stable releases [v0.15.1](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.15.1) and [v0.15.2](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.15.2). v0.15.1 adds updater artifact budgets and required-file checks, fixes provisioning and sensor-loss behavior, and exposes journal logs without `sudo`; v0.15.2 adds configurable velocity limits, eases into a recorded rest pose before cutting torque, and pauses the active policy while the robot is held. Open pull requests and `daemon-dev-*` builds remain unreleased work.
- Reviewed the official policy-set [v6 update](https://huggingface.co/pollen-robotics/microduck-policies/commit/d30a21695085d6685d8c19ce63521a7b8e069b2f) and [v7 update](https://huggingface.co/pollen-robotics/microduck-policies/commit/6677ec007142bbaf05123a468f6ae58d13808786). Their authors report real-robot validation: v6 retrains sit/stand against backward tipping from abrupt seated head motion, and v7 makes body roll/pitch steerable while the VelStand gait is standing still. The v7 source explicitly says body-height crouch was not learned.
- Re-reviewed the official `microduck_rl` default `develop` branch at [273afe0](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck_rl/commit/273afe0b31c4ab365b9ff806a927b63ac92b5ddd). It now documents the v7 VelStand training lineage and Hub publication path; this is branch state, not a separately tagged training release.
- Updated [Microduck Playground](https://github.com/Vottivott/microduck-playground/commit/f010ef1) after its raised-platform long-jump and backflip release. It publishes policies, checkpoints and reproduction notes, while labeling both as selected simulation footage, leaving hardware unvalidated and warning that backflip landings may break the robot.
- Updated [Microduck Lab](https://github.com/jonathanhawkins/microduck-lab/commit/bbf0326) for its browser-based multi-robot worlds, including multi-duck soccer and tidying behaviors plus additional robot bodies. The reviewed claims are measured simulation results; its own notes keep the hardware gap explicit.
- Added [DuckKit v1.37.0](https://github.com/craigm26/duckkit/releases/tag/v1.37.0), a zero-dependency Swift implementation of Microduck policy inference, observation, kinematics and RPC contracts. The maintainer reports 347 offline tests across Linux and macOS, but no physical-device validation.
- Added [quackd v0.17.1](https://github.com/rokbenko/quackd/releases/tag/v0.17.1), a goal-directed CLI and MCP bridge with Microduck mock, 2-D and MuJoCo paths. Its own status table says the Microduck JSON-RPC hardware backend has only pinned-name fake validation, so it remains `hardware-unverified`.
- Flagged the [XGO Duck connector correction](https://github.com/LuwuDynamics/xgoduck_hardware/blob/63b1c45/PCBA/README.md): the earlier Arduino Uno Q board files reverse GND and signal on U3, U4 and U5. The revised source files and old-board cable workaround are documented, but full ERC/DRC, copper-connectivity and physical-rework validation are still not complete.
- Deferred four official Microduck Arena reference-policy repositories from separate catalog entries. Their ONNX files and manifests are public on Hugging Face, but the model cards' claimed reproduction source, `pollen-robotics/microduck-challenges`, returned 404 during review. Also deferred newly surfaced single-policy uploads and demo-only Spaces until provenance, evaluation and practical reader value can be checked beyond their publisher descriptions.

本次核心更新：官方稳定运行时升至 v0.15.2，策略集升至 v7，并保留作者真机验证与下蹲未学会等边界；官方训练默认分支补充 v7 训练谱系与 Hub 发布流程。社区侧更新 Playground 与 Microduck Lab 的仿真能力，新增 DuckKit 和 quackd，同时明确二者尚无 Microduck 真机验证。XGO Duck 旧板 U3/U4/U5 的 GND/信号接反属于上电前必须处理的安全勘误。Microduck Arena 参考策略因复现仓库当前不可访问而暂不单独收录。

## 2026-09-29 — Runtime telemetry, new policy demo and hardware progress / 运行时遥测、策略演示与硬件进展

- Reviewed official stable releases [v0.14.2](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.14.2), [v0.14.3](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.14.3), [v0.14.4](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.14.4) and [v0.15.0](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.15.0). The stable runtime now reports joint velocity and load each tick, documents server-side agent control over the rendezvous lane, improves update-source and central-identity health reporting, and can reboot a tripped servo over Bluetooth. Newer `daemon-dev-*` artifacts were treated as prereleases, not stable capabilities.
- Updated [Microduck Playground](https://github.com/Vottivott/microduck-playground/commit/4289c23686) after its documented chimney-climb simulation release. It publishes climb, enter and exit policies plus a selected rollout, while explicitly leaving hardware validation open.
- Changed [Microduck replica](https://github.com/fanhao375/microduck-replica) from `experimental` to `hardware-demo-reported`. The HD-1910 route now has an assembled build, pose control and measured 14-joint readback; the source explicitly says the complete IMU, inference and action loop and walking have not been accepted.
- Added [XGO Duck hardware](https://github.com/LuwuDynamics/xgoduck_hardware), its [Arduino Uno Q runtime](https://github.com/LuwuDynamics/xgoduck_runtime_arduino) and [training fork](https://github.com/LuwuDynamics/xgoduck_rl) as one hardware route. Author-published footage demonstrates walking, get-up and pick policies. Its Uno Q, Feetech 1910 and QMI8658 configuration is a downstream adaptation, not the official Microduck hardware.
- The official `microduck_rl` main branch had no commits dated inside the review window, so no training claim or review date was advanced.
- Deferred XDuck because the available author demonstration reports a first walking result but no public build repository, and explicitly notes unresolved structural-strength work. Other newly created repositories found through GitHub search were deferred when they were empty, only days old, lacked reproducible evidence, or made performance claims that could not yet be cross-checked.

本次核心更新：官方稳定运行时升至 v0.15.0，增加关节速度与负载遥测、服务端 Agent 通路文档和健康检查改进；Microduck Playground 新增烟囱式攀爬仿真，但仍未验证真机；HD-1910 复刻已有装机、姿态控制和关节回读证据，状态升为 `hardware-demo-reported`，但完整控制环和行走仍待验证；新收录 XGO Duck 的 Arduino Uno Q 硬件、运行时与训练路线。XDuck 和其他缺少可复现证据的新项目暂不收录。

## 2026-09-21 — Runtime release and new community routes / 运行时发布与新社区路线

- Reviewed the official [daemon v0.14.0](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.14.0) and [v0.14.1](https://github.com/pollen-robotics/microduck/releases/tag/daemon-v0.14.1) releases. The first replaces the fake simulator path with the real runtime daemons against MuJoCo and adds a browser policy playground; the second exposes phone stand-up and policy-start controls and fixes controller reconnection behavior.
- Added the official releases page as the stable-version and artifact trail; development builds remain a separate class.
- Added [Microduck Web](https://github.com/TonyRuan/microduck-web) as a community-reported local browser simulation route with touch controls and compatible ONNX policy import. Its own documentation explicitly limits the project to simulation rather than physical hardware control.
- Added [OpenMicroDuck](https://github.com/SaberOnGo/open-microduck) as a bilingual community guide. Its evidence labels and unresolved hardware questions remain part of the recommendation.
- Added [Fanduck](https://github.com/7757/fanduck) as `hardware-demo-reported`: the maintainer publishes physical-build photos and clips plus versioned BOM, print and board packages. This is author evidence, not an independent reproduction, and the upstream-derived model carries non-commercial terms.
- Deferred several same-week repositories that were empty, extremely new, tied to an external service, or based on simulation findings not yet independently cross-checked. Deferral is not a negative verdict; they can be reconsidered as reproducible evidence accumulates.

本次核心更新：官方 v0.14.0/0.14.1 将真实运行时守护进程与 MuJoCo 仿真、手机起立和策略启动串联起来；新收录浏览器仿真、双语指南与有作者实机证据的复刻项目。同期内容为空、刚创建或实验结论尚未复核的项目暂不收录。
